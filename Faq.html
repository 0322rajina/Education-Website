Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions Answered

What is EduPlatform and how does it work?

EduPlatform is an online learning portal offering hundreds of courses across programming, design, business, and marketing. Simply browse courses, enroll, and start learning at your own pace.

How can I enroll in a course?

Choose a course from the Courses page, click the "Enroll" button, create an account or login, and complete the payment process. Once done, you can start the course immediately.

Do I get a certificate after completion?

Yes! After completing a course successfully, you will receive a digital certificate that you can download or share on LinkedIn and other platforms.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and local payment gateways depending on your country.

Can I get a refund if I’m not satisfied?

Yes, we offer a 14-day refund policy on most courses. Refund requests must be submitted through your account dashboard.
